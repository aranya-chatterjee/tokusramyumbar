How Toku's happened

Deepak and Kanishka had the corporate life all figured out: the desk, the deadlines, the Monday-morning meetings. So they did the sensible thing and walked away from all of it.

Their first leap was Berrylicious, proof that two ex-corporate folks could build something of their own. Then came the next big thing, a little louder and a lot slurpier: Toku's, Madhya Pradesh's very first ramen bar.

Somewhere along the way Aryan pulled up a chair and joined as their third partner. Three friends, one wall of noodles, and a city that keeps coming back for the second bowl.
